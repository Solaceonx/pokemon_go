# Full-state validation for creator footage

A consistency verdict is separate from source accuracy. A correct source observation can coexist with a wrong default build, an edited/censored ending, or a visible win that leaves surviving opposing Pokémon. Retain the existing strict verdict, source evidence, unknowns and failed checks separately.

The intended output is a timeline of observable state plus explicit uncertainty for hidden state. HP is a fraction interval measured from the physical bar; CP alone does not provide exact maximum HP or IVs. A zero-pixel reading with positive values inside its uncertainty interval is not a confirmed faint. A late replacement never establishes an earlier exact faint time by itself. Source party decreases, coherent disappearance and subsequent life evidence must agree. A result establishes the observed outcome, not extra knockouts or an observed surrender cause.

Each event needs source PTS/frame provenance and separate occurrence/confirmation times. Retrospective form resolution and future replacement evidence must not leak into decision-time features. Generic observed moves such as Weather Ball should retain their generic identity and unresolved variants; an ambiguous type must not erase the observed release or choose a default move slot. Model-based estimates must retain their assumptions.

Evaluate independently on battles unused for tuning. Freeze source-only labels before inspecting predictions. Report per-event precision/recall, extra and missing events, timing error, per-field accuracy, intervals and unknown coverage. Report battle-level uncertainty margins and the small-sample limits. Existing six reference clips are development/regression data. Any newly evaluated examples used to choose fixes subsequently become development data and need another fresh evaluation.

Readiness must be explicit for each use and interval. Event analysis requires verified identities, releases, shield use and faint/termination evidence. Health analysis additionally requires attributable, validated HP intervals with gaps preserved. Decision/habit analysis additionally requires useful energy bounds, correct switch causes/clocks and stages, initial-state coverage and causal knowledge timestamps. Unknown values do not count as verified fields. A field-only or event-only success does not make a full battle decision-ready.

Current implementation and scores are experimental. Full-state, boundary/uniqueness, useful energy, stage/ability and timer accuracy remain to be established; consult the dated Task46 results in status.md. Private work, media and draft source labels are under `.vod/task46-fullstate-20261118`; typed reproducible reports belong under `runs` and the established creator-record format. No images, video, unrestricted OCR or trainer names belong in Git.


`scripts/recording/evaluate_creator_fullstate.py` scores source events with `--event-source source`, explicit unknown shield decisions, point timing at0/1/3seconds and wide timing-interval counts. It records prediction hashes, labels and evaluator versions. Field reports include usable accuracy and coverage with battle-cluster bootstrap margins; all-perfect development samples and six-battle intervals are not an independent reliability guarantee.

`scripts/recording/state_readiness.py --root games/creators --reference-report <source-report.json> --out <report.json>` produces per-record evidence coverage plus separately verified event counts, timing and all shield decisions. Verification requires the exact exported hashes. A wide faint interval cannot pass the narrow timing gate. Missing/unknown/pending fields and time gaps remain in coverage denominators. Current full-state certification is unavailable; the assessor does not turn a model consistency pass into source verification.

A creator record whose registered visible form is absent from the1500rankings can now publish source-only observations with `model-support.json` status unavailable. The model verdict stays failed, model streams contain no predictions, and prior model files are archived locally. Game-record output preserves observed events and explicit unknowns rather than substituting an ordinary species or an assumed enhanced-move energy meter. This separates source processing success from simulator support; neither certifies full state.

Fourth-source metadata selection and source-only boundary corrections are in data/creator_fullstate_fourth_eval_split_v1.json. Six source references and 224 native frame witnesses were frozen before the first v20 predictions. The original overwide local Reis cut remains retained and excluded. A later source audit corrected one assistant label: Cramorant was not independently confirmed fainted, and two opposing party icons remained alive. Original labels and first scores stay immutable; corrected1 scores are separate and later tuning is development. No video, frames, trainer names or unrestricted OCR belong in Git.

As of v21g, the 24 development battles match 245 charged releases, 87 shield spends and 86 faints, with no extras. The corrected fourth-source six match 60, 21 and 20 respectively. These event results do not certify full state. The same fourth-source sampled health reference has 15 correct usable readings out of 24 possible fields; form coverage is 12 correct usable readings out of 21 anchors, across only two battles containing form labels. Unknown initial intact forms are retained. See the typed development reports for denominators, battle-level margins and limitations.

Ability-caption acquisition serializes only exact game labels, confidence, pixel bounds and source PTS. Two distinct frames are required. Header attribution may wait for the first matching physical species/CP header to return. Occurrence, recognition availability and attribution confirmation are distinct times; none may expose a fact earlier in the causal timeline. A buffer reread triggered by a later observation remains unavailable before that trigger. Rounded OCR timestamps must resolve to the matching physical frame, rather than being handled with a broad timing tolerance.

The latest completed all-82 consistency replay has 27 strict passes, compared with the original 17 and v15's 28. The v21g2 replay is in progress in a separate copy; the public catalog must not be rebuilt from partial or mixed results. Four unavailable models retain null metrics. No full-state-ready record is certified. Energy, stages, switch clocks, gaps and a new untouched source test remain open.
